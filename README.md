# GEC Uplink

**The desktop companion for the Goblin Engineering Company addons.** One small
app that keeps your WoW addons up to date and your data flowing to your
personal dashboard — so the numbers you earn in-game follow you out of it.

Built by the [Goblin Engineering Company](https://goblineng.co).

## What it does

**Delivers your addons.** Uplink finds your World of Warcraft installs
automatically — Retail, Classic, PTR, every flavor, on every drive — and
installs, updates, and pins the GEC addons per install. New release ships,
Uplink has it in seconds. No more downloading zips or dragging folders.

**Syncs your data.** Everything the addons record in-game — fishing sessions,
farming hauls, catches, skill-ups — syncs to your account on
[goblineng.co](https://goblineng.co): your personal stats pages, session
history, and fishing analytics, plus anonymous community datasets like the
zone atlas that get better for everyone with every contribution.

**Stays out of the way.** It lives in your menu bar (macOS) or system tray
(Windows/Linux) as a compact dropdown. Open the full window when you want to
dig in; otherwise it just quietly does its job.

## Highlights

- Auto-detects every WoW install and flavor; add unusual paths manually
- Per-install addon selection with per-addon release channels and version pins
- One-click **Update all**, with clear what's-new release notes
- Secure device pairing — a short code links Uplink to your account; the
  credential is stored privately on your device, never in the app's config
- Release channels: public for everyone, pre-release and workshop early
  access for supporters
- Themeable to match the workshop: Workshop, Blueprint, or Parchment
- Runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux — including the Steam Deck

## Your data

Your synced data is **yours**: it's visible only to your account, and your
characters appear publicly only if you mark them public. Community datasets
use anonymous totals only. Syncing is optional — Uplink is a fine addon
manager all by itself.
